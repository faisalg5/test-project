# Episode 1: "The Shiny Berry"

**Lesson:** Sharing makes good things even better.
**Length:** 4:00 | **Scenes:** 16 | **Words of narration/dialogue:** about 430

**Style suffix for every image prompt (S):**
`soft 3D cartoon style, Pixar-like storybook look, warm golden lighting, rounded friendly shapes, big expressive eyes, pastel and warm colors, cozy forest setting, child-friendly, no text`

**Character shorthand (use full descriptions in every prompt for consistency):**
- **FIN** = young orange fox cub, cream chest and cheeks, big green eyes, oversized ears, white-tipped fluffy tail, small blue scarf
- **PIP** = small fluffy white rabbit, one floppy ear, pink nose, tiny yellow backpack
- **MILA** = round brown owl, cream belly, amber eyes, tiny round yellow glasses, green leaf behind ear tuft
- **BUMBLE** = chubby light-brown bear, red-and-white checkered vest, kind sleepy eyes

**Visual:** Sunrise over Maple Hollow. Camera glides through the trees to Fin's round door.
**Audio:** Opening theme song plays.
**Narrator:** *"Welcome to Maple Hollow! Let's see what Fin the fox is up to today."*

## SCENE 2: Fin wakes up (0:20–0:35)
**Visual:** Fin stretches and yawns, tail wagging, then puts on his blue scarf.
**Fin:** *"Good morning, Maple Hollow! Today is going to be a great day!"*
**Image prompt:** `FIN standing at the round door of his burrow, stretching arms up with a big smile, morning sunlight, S`
**Video prompt:** `the fox cub stretches, yawns, then smiles and wags his tail, soft cartoon motion`

## SCENE 3: Walking to the river (0:35–0:55)
**Visual:** Fin skips along the path past flowers.
**Narrator:** *"Fin skipped down the path to the river, humming his favorite tune."*
**Image prompt:** `FIN skipping happily along a winding path lined with wildflowers, blue scarf flying in the breeze, river in the background, S`
**Video prompt:** `the fox cub skips along the flower path, scarf bouncing, camera follows from the side`

## SCENE 4: The discovery (0:55–1:20)
**Visual:** Fin gasps: a giant, glowing berry sparkles on a bush.
**Fin:** *"Whoa! It's the biggest, shiniest berry I have ever seen!"*
**Image prompt:** `FIN with wide amazed eyes staring at one enormous glowing red berry on a green bush by the river, magical sparkles around the berry, S`
**Video prompt:** `the berry glows and sparkles softly, the fox cub's eyes widen and he leans in with wonder`

## SCENE 5: Pip arrives (1:20–1:45)
**Visual:** Pip hops in, sniffing the air.
**Pip:** *"Hi Fin! Ooh, what a yummy-looking berry! Can I have a little bite?"*
**Image prompt:** `PIP the rabbit hopping happily toward FIN and the glowing berry, hopeful smile, riverside meadow, S`
**Video prompt:** `the white rabbit hops into frame with bouncy motion, nose twitching, ears bobbing`

## SCENE 6: The mistake (1:45–2:10)
**Visual:** Fin hides the berry behind his back and frowns.
**Fin:** *"No! It's MINE! I found it first!"*
**Narrator:** *"Pip's ears drooped. Slowly, she hopped away."*
**Image prompt:** `FIN holding the glowing berry tightly behind his back with a stubborn pout, PIP walking away in the background with drooping ears, S`
**Video prompt:** `the fox cub turns away hugging the berry, the rabbit slowly hops away with drooping ears`

## SCENE 7: The first bite (2:10–2:30)
**Visual:** Fin sits alone and takes a big bite. His smile fades.
**Narrator:** *"Fin took a big bite. But something felt… strange."*
**Fin:** *"Hmm… why doesn't it taste as good as I thought?"*
**Image prompt:** `FIN sitting alone on a river rock holding a half-eaten berry, puzzled sad expression, quiet river scene, muted warm light, S`
**Video prompt:** `the fox cub chews slowly then looks down with a puzzled face, gentle breeze moves his scarf`

## SCENE 8: Fin sees Pip (2:30–2:40)
**Visual:** In the distance, Pip sits alone under a tree.
**Image prompt:** `wide shot: FIN in the foreground looking across the meadow toward PIP sitting alone under a maple tree, gentle sadness, soft golden light, S`
**Video prompt:** `slow push-in on the fox cub looking across the meadow, leaves drifting down`

## SCENE 9: Mila appears (2:40–3:05)
**Visual:** Mila glides down onto a branch beside Fin.
**Mila:** *"Hello, Fin. You look a little quiet. What happened?"*
**Fin:** *"I didn't share, and now the berry doesn't taste good."*
**Mila:** *"Hmm. Why do you think that is?"*
**Image prompt:** `MILA the owl perched on a branch next to FIN, kind curious expression, FIN looking thoughtful, river and meadow behind, S`
**Video prompt:** `the owl glides down and lands on the branch, tilts her head kindly, the fox cub looks up`

## SCENE 10: The realization (3:05–3:15)
**Visual:** Fin's ears perk up, a lightbulb-style sparkle.
**Fin:** *"Maybe… because I was eating it all alone?"*
**Mila (smiling):** *"Sometimes good things taste best with friends."*
**Image prompt:** `FIN with a sudden realization, ears perked up, small sparkles above his head, MILA smiling beside him, S`
**Video prompt:** `the fox cub's ears pop up with a happy gasp, sparkles twinkle, the owl nods with a smile`

## SCENE 11: Fin runs to Pip (3:15–3:25)
**Visual:** Fin bolts across the meadow holding the berry.
**Fin:** *"Pip! Wait! Come back!"*
**Image prompt:** `FIN running joyfully across a sunny flower meadow holding the half berry high, scarf streaming behind him, S`
**Video prompt:** `the fox cub runs across the meadow with bouncy energy, camera tracks alongside`

## SCENE 12: Sharing (3:25–3:40)
**Visual:** Fin offers half the berry to Pip. Pip's eyes light up.
**Fin:** *"I'm sorry, Pip. Would you like to share the berry with me?"*
**Pip:** *"Oh, yes please! Thank you, Fin!"*
**Image prompt:** `FIN holding out half a glowing berry toward PIP, PIP smiling with sparkling happy eyes, warm sunset-golden light, S`
**Video prompt:** `the fox cub holds out the berry, the rabbit's eyes light up and she hops with joy`

## SCENE 13: Lesson moment (3:40–3:50)
**Visual:** Fin and Pip eat together. The berry glows even brighter and Bumble waves from afar.
**Audio:** Lesson jingle.
**Fin (giggling):** *"Wow! It tastes SO much better now!"*
**Narrator:** *"Sharing makes good things even better."*
**Image prompt:** `FIN and PIP sitting side by side sharing the glowing berry, laughing, BUMBLE the bear waving cheerfully in the background, sparkles in the air, S`
**Video prompt:** `the fox cub and rabbit laugh together while sparkles float around them, the bear waves in the background`

## SCENE 14: Viewer question (3:50–3:55)
**Visual:** Fin turns to the camera, friendly close-up.
**Fin (to camera):** *"Have you ever shared something special? What would YOU do?"*
**Image prompt:** `close-up of FIN looking directly at the viewer with a warm friendly smile, one paw raised, soft blurred forest background, S`
**Video prompt:** `the fox cub looks at the camera, smiles and gives a small wave`

## SCENE 15: Goodbye (3:55–4:00)
**Visual:** All characters wave as the sun sets.
**Audio:** Closing song begins.

- Give every line a full second of breathing room after it. Kids need time to absorb.
