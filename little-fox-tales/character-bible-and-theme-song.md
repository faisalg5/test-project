# Little Fox Tales: Character Prompts and Theme Song

## Global style prompt (paste at the end of EVERY image prompt)

> soft 3D cartoon style, Pixar-like storybook look, warm golden lighting, rounded friendly shapes, big expressive eyes, pastel and warm colors, cozy forest setting, child-friendly, clean background, high detail, no text

**Negative / avoid:** scary, dark, realistic fur, sharp teeth, extra limbs, extra fingers, distorted face, text, watermark, harsh shadows

---

## Characters

### Fin the Fox (hero)
- **Age/personality:** 6 years old, curious, impulsive, big-hearted. Learns by making small mistakes.
- **Look:** small orange fox cub, cream-white chest and cheeks, white-tipped fluffy tail, oversized ears, big green eyes, a small **blue scarf** (his signature item, never remove it).
- **Image prompt:**
  `Fin, a cute young orange fox cub with a cream-white chest and cheeks, big round green eyes, oversized ears, fluffy tail with a white tip, wearing a small blue scarf, standing on two legs in a friendly pose, smiling, full body, [GLOBAL STYLE]`
- **Expression sheet prompt:**
  `character expression sheet of Fin the fox cub with blue scarf: happy, sad, surprised, curious, guilty, proud, six poses on a plain cream background, consistent character design, [GLOBAL STYLE]`
- **Voice:** bright, energetic child voice, slightly high-pitched.

### Mila the Owl (wise friend)
- **Personality:** calm, playful, asks questions instead of giving answers.
- **Look:** round soft-brown owl with a cream belly, big amber eyes, tiny round yellow glasses, a small green leaf tucked behind one ear-tuft.
- **Image prompt:**
  `Mila, a cute round brown owl with a cream belly, big amber eyes, tiny round yellow glasses, a small green leaf behind her ear tuft, perched on a branch, warm smile, [GLOBAL STYLE]`
- **Voice:** warm, gentle, slow, motherly.

### Bumble the Bear (gentle giant)
- **Personality:** big, clumsy, kind, a little shy.
- **Look:** chubby light-brown bear cub, round tummy, small round ears, a red-and-white checkered vest, sleepy kind eyes.
- **Image prompt:**
  `Bumble, a big chubby light-brown bear with a round belly, small round ears, kind sleepy eyes, wearing a red and white checkered vest, standing and waving, [GLOBAL STYLE]`
- **Voice:** deep, slow, friendly.

### Pip the Rabbit (fast and anxious)
- **Personality:** speedy, nervous, very kind.
- **Look:** small white rabbit with pale pink inner ears (one ear slightly floppy), pink nose, a tiny yellow backpack.
- **Image prompt:**
  `Pip, a small fluffy white rabbit with one floppy ear and one upright ear, pink nose, big shiny dark eyes, wearing a tiny yellow backpack, gentle worried smile, [GLOBAL STYLE]`
- **Voice:** quick, soft, slightly trembly.

## Setting: Maple Hollow

- **Master environment prompt:**
  `Maple Hollow, a cozy storybook forest village, giant maple trees with tiny round doors and windows, a sparkling river with stepping stones, wildflower meadow, warm afternoon sunlight, floating pollen sparkles, [GLOBAL STYLE]`
- **Recurring spots:** the Berry Bushes by the river, Mila's Tree Library, Fin's Burrow (round door under a big root), the Meadow.

## Theme song: "Little Fox, Little Fox"

**Style prompt for an AI music generator:**
`cheerful upbeat kids' theme song, ukulele, glockenspiel and hand claps, simple catchy melody, 110 BPM, warm friendly female and child vocals, singalong, 25 seconds, ends on a happy sting`

### Opening theme (about 20 seconds, plays at 0:00)

> 🎵 *Little Fox, Little Fox, where will you go?*
> *Through Maple Hollow, where the wild flowers grow!*
> *Hop with your friends, and give it a try,*
> *Every little tale makes you shine up high!*
> *Little Fox, Little Fox, ready to play,*
> *Let's learn something new today!* 🎵

### Closing song (about 15 seconds, plays at 3:45)

> 🎵 *Good-bye, good-bye, from Maple Hollow,*
> *Be kind and brave wherever you go!*
> *Little Fox, Little Fox, see you next week,*
> *A brand-new tale is all we seek!* 🎵

### Lesson jingle (5 seconds, plays at the lesson moment)
> 🎵 *That's the lesson, big and small, / a little kindness helps us all!* 🎵

## Voice and audio notes
- Narrator: a warm, slow storyteller, a bit like a parent at bedtime. Keep pace around 120 words per minute.
- Gentle sound effects only: a soft "boing" for hops, twinkle for magic, birdsong ambience. No loud sounds.
- Keep music soft under dialogue (about -20 dB).
